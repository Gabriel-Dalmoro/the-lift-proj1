# The Lift — image prompts for Nano Banana Pro

Goal: take real photos of The Lift as it is today and turn them into what it **could become**: the same room, the same windows, the same view over Lyon, but covered in a lush urban jungle.

## How to use

1. Upload **one real photo of The Lift** as the base (image 1). This is the room the AI must keep.
2. Optionally add **one mood photo** (image 2) from the reference sites below. Say clearly in the prompt that image 2 is for mood only.
3. Paste the **master prompt** first, then the **shot prompt** for the image you want.
4. Generate 3–4 versions, keep the one where the room is still clearly The Lift.
5. Save it with the **filename** given, send it to me, I drop it in.

Tip: if the AI moves walls or changes the windows, add at the end: *"You changed the architecture. Start again from image 1 and only add plants and light."*

## What Sabrina's references actually look like

**Twentysix Budapest** (her main reference, ambiance page)
- Plants at **every height**: big palms and banana trees on the floor, bushy plants at table height, vines climbing up walls and balconies, plants hanging from the ceiling.
- The green is **dense and layered**, you never see an empty wall.
- At night: warm small lights, lamps and string lights glowing through the leaves, deep green shadows, golden pools of light on tables.
- Busy with people, lively, not a museum.
- Their cocktail photos: dark background, smoke, colourful glassware, a warm lamp.

**Dar Ahlam / Thierry Teyssier** (her reference for hospitality)
- Earthy and warm: terracotta, clay, sand, palm trees, golden hour.
- Calm and poetic, natural materials, linen, nothing shiny or "bling".
- Simple human moments: a hand held out, a small gift, a quiet welcome.

So the target is: **Twentysix's lush, layered jungle** inside **The Lift's real room**, lit with **Dar Ahlam's warm, earthy light**.

## Master prompt (paste first, every time)

```
Image 1 is a real photo of The Lift, a rooftop restaurant on the 8th floor in Lyon, France. Keep image 1 as the base: keep the exact same architecture, walls, ceiling, windows, floor, bar, furniture layout, camera position, lens and perspective. Keep the real view through the windows. Do not move, add or remove walls, windows or furniture.

Transform the room into a lush urban jungle restaurant, inspired by Twentysix Budapest: abundant, layered, almost exuberant greenery at every height. Large palms, banana plants, monstera, ficus and olive trees in big planters on the floor; dense bushy plants between tables; trailing pothos and ivy hanging from the ceiling and shelves; vines climbing the walls and columns. The plants must look real, healthy and professionally installed, in natural terracotta and dark stone planters, never plastic.

Light: warm and golden, late afternoon sun or warm evening lamps, soft shadows, a slight haze. Colours: deep jungle greens, terracotta, amber, warm brass, cream. Mood: luxurious, immersive, "an oasis eight floors above Lyon", warm and welcoming, never cold or stiff.

Photorealistic, high-end editorial interior photography, shot on a full-frame camera, natural depth of field, fine film grain. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no signage. No white tablecloths, no cold blue light.
```

If you add a mood photo as image 2, add this line:
```
Image 2 is only a mood reference for the density of plants and the warmth of the light. Do not copy its architecture or its objects.
```

## Shot prompts (one per image on the site)

Sizes: **portrait 4:5** works for phone and laptop columns. **Landscape 16:9** for full-width images. For the hero, make **both** a 9:16 (phone) and a 16:9 (laptop) version if you can.
